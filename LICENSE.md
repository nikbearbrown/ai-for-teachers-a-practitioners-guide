This applies to commercial and noncommercial uses alike. Scraping, mirroring, or ingesting the text for ML training purposes is not permitted under fair use and is not permitted under this license.

This restriction exists for a specific reason. The book's argument is that the productive division of labor between humans and AI requires explicit boundaries about what AI is and is not allowed to do with cognitive material that constitutes learning. Allowing the book itself to be ingested into general-purpose training corpora without consent or compensation — and then reflected back into student-facing tools without the author's voice, framework, or limits intact — would contradict the argument by example.
