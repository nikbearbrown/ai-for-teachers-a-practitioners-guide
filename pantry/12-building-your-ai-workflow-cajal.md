## Figure 12.4 — Prompt Quality with vs. without Maintenance

**Priority: Critical.** The maintenance argument is the chapter's least intuitive claim. A decay-vs-snapback chart makes it visible in one glance.

### Block 1 — Illustrae paste block

A line chart. X-axis horizontal: months 0–24 with Black `#000000` 1pt tick marks at 0, 6, 12, 18, 24. Y-axis vertical: prompt output quality, scale 0 to 1.0, with a Black 1pt tick at 1.0 baseline. A Vermillion `#D55E00` 1.5pt smooth curve starts at (0, 1.0) and decays monotonically downward, passing roughly through (12, 0.60) and ending near (24, 0.30) — the no-maintenance trajectory. A Blue `#0072B2` 1.5pt curve starts at (0, 1.0), decays gently to about (6, 0.85), then snaps vertically back to 1.0 at month 6, decays again to about (12, 0.85), snaps back, repeats at months 18 and 24 — the maintained trajectory holding near 1.0 across the full span. Two small Orange `#E69F00` filled diamonds mark the snap-back events at months 6, 12, 18 — the 30-minute maintenance calendar entries. Faint Sky Blue `#56B4E9` 15% horizontal band shades the y-axis region above 0.85 indicating the "asset" zone. White background, flat vector, single-column 89mm.
