## Figure 14.3 — Storage Strength vs. Retrieval Strength

**Priority: Critical.** The Bjork mechanism that makes the Bastani finding inevitable.

### Block 1 — Illustrae paste block

A two-line chart. X-axis: time, from study session to "two weeks later," with Black `#000000` 1pt tick marks at *immediate*, *5 min*, *1 day*, *1 week*, *2 weeks*. Y-axis: strength, scale 0 to 1.0. Two curves. *Retrieval strength* (Vermillion `#D55E00` 1.5pt smooth curve): starts very high at the immediate point (≈0.95), decays steeply, ending low at two weeks (≈0.25). *Storage strength* (Blue `#0072B2` 1.5pt smooth curve): starts low (≈0.30), rises gradually with retrieval-practice events, stays roughly flat or rises slightly, ending higher than retrieval strength at two weeks. A small Orange `#E69F00` filled diamond on the x-axis between *immediate* and *5 min* marks the "feels-fluent" zone, with a faint Orange 15% opacity vertical band shading that region. A small Bluish Green `#009E73` filled diamond marks the *2 weeks* point on the storage curve as "what the exam measures." Curves cross at roughly the *1 day* mark — the moment when the introspective signal stops tracking durable learning. White background, flat vector, single-column 89mm.
