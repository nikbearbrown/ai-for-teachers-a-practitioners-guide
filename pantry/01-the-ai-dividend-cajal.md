## Zone Map

- **MC:** The dividend is a function of which task-category sort you do, with phase gates calibrated to the category — not a uniform per-task speedup. The reinvestment is a decision, not a consequence.
- **VG:** Three-zone task taxonomy (AI-Appropriate / Hybrid / Human-Required) as a spatial sort. The 5.9-hour number with its scope conditions named as outer boundary callouts.
- **PQ:** 5.9 hrs/week (ad hoc, weekly users, post-learning-curve). ~16–17 hrs/week (systematic ceiling, modeled). 25 min/week lesson-prep saving (NFER RCT). 13–14 hrs/week (worked example, year-two ceiling).
