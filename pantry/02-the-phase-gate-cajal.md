## Zone Map

- **MC:** A gate has three components (upstream / operation / downstream); without all three it is a phrase. The click is not the operation. PCK is irreducibly human. The jagged frontier means uniform policies are wrong on both sides.
- **VG:** Three-component gate anatomy (linear three-node flow). Jagged frontier (irregular boundary in task-space). PCK Venn (subject-matter knowledge ∩ pedagogical knowledge). Twelve-gate cluster (risk-tier-encoded chip layout).
- **PQ:** 99.4% approval rate (three-second click). 48% practice improvement / -17 percentage-point exam decrement (Bastani unscaffolded). 127% practice improvement, no decrement (Bastani scaffolded). 25% / +40% (Dell'Acqua jagged frontier in-frontier). 19 percentage-point error increase (out-of-frontier). 25 minutes calibration vs 3–5 hours rework (Gate 1 time-cost).
